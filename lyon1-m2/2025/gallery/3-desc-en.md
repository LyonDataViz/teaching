[ChessViz](https://viz.maxime-antoine.eu/)

This project focuses on the analytical study of chess openings using data from games played on the online platform [Lichess.org](https://database.lichess.org/).

The first visualization presents the most played opening moves, allowing users to explore different openings and their variations. The second visualization shows the win rate and popularity of various openings. Finally, the last visualization analyzes the impact of move accuracy during the opening phase and the rest of the game on the final outcome.

It is possible to filter the data based on the type of games (rapid, blitz, bullet), player skill level (Elo), piece color, and the opening used. Additionally, all visualizations are interconnected to enable interactive data exploration.
