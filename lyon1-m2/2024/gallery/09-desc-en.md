# Analysis of Personal Data from the Health App

## Project Goal
The goal of this project is to analyze and compare personal data collected through the Health app on iPhones of our group members. We aim to better understand their habits related to physical activity, sleep, and well-being through interactive visualizations and engaging storytelling.

### Key Questions
We address the following questions:  
1. How does physical activity vary between group members?  
2. How do sleep cycles differ among group members?  
3. Do activity levels influence sleep quality?  
4. How do health behaviors evolve over a week or month?  

## Main Visualizations
We developed six visualizations to enable detailed analysis:  

1. **Line Chart: Step Comparison**  
   Compares the number of steps taken by users year over year. The year can be switched using interactive buttons.  

2. **Bubble Chart: Distance Analysis**  
   Displays the relationship between distance traveled and calories burned, with data segmented by year.  

3. **Line Chart: Calorie Burn Analysis**  
   Shows the evolution of calories burned per month, with an option to switch the year.  

4. **Bar Chart: Sleep Hours Comparison**  
   Represents the number of sleep hours per month. Clicking on a bar reveals a detailed breakdown of daily sleep for that month.  

5. **Bubble Chart: Sleep Hours**  
   Visualizes the relationship between weekly sleep hours and steps taken.  

6. **Radial Chart: Distance and Sleep**  
   Presents weekly distance traveled and sleep hours in a radial format.  

## Dynamic Features
The visualizations dynamically adapt when the user changes the year using the buttons. This ensures a seamless experience and coherent storytelling, with updated descriptions for each visualization.
